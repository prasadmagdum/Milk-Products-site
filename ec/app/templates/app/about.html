About Us


Magdum Dairy is a Kolhapur-based dairy dedicated to delivering fresh, pure, and high-quality milk and dairy products to local households. Rooted in the rich agricultural tradition of Kolhapur, we focus on providing farm-fresh products while maintaining strict hygiene and quality standards. We are committed to serving the people of Kolhapur with natural and nutritious dairy products, building trust through consistency, freshness, and reliability.



Mission


Our mission is to provide the people of Kolhapur with safe, fresh, and affordable dairy products while supporting local farmers and sustainable practices. We aim to:

  • Deliver pure and unadulterated milk across Kolhapur
  • Maintain high standards of hygiene and quality
  • Support and empower local dairy farmers
  • Ensure timely and reliable service to every customer
  • Promote healthy living through fresh dairy nutrition
